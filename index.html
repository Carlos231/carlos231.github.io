A stroke counter that knows golf

One tap per shot

Tap + for each stroke, putt, and penalty. No typing and no menus, so your phone goes straight back in your pocket.

Always know your score

Your total and score vs par update with every tap, with the right name for each hole: birdie, par, bogey.

Free, with unlimited rounds

No account and no ads. Stats, a shareable scorecard, and spreadsheet export are part of the free app.

How it works

  1. Start a round

    Quick Start gives you 18 holes, the Front 9, or the Back 9 in one tap. Or name the course and set the par for each hole.

  2. Tap as you play

    Count strokes, putts, and penalties on each hole, then move to the next. Your round saves as you go.

  3. See how you did

    Get a round summary and a classic scorecard to share. Playing the same course again? Copy the round and its pars carry over.

Questions golfers ask

Does Golf Caddie count putts separately from strokes?

Yes. Each hole has three counters: strokes, putts, and penalties. Your score for the hole is the three added together, shown against par. New to this? See how to track putts.

Can I fix a mistake?

Yes. Tap the minus button to take a tap back, or go back to any earlier hole and change it. You can still make changes after the round is finished.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. {{ gc.short_name }} works on iPhone alone, and scoring on iPhone is free. The Apple Watch app is optional and part of {{ gc.short_name }} Pro.
