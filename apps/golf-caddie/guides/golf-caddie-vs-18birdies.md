## Is 18Birdies free?

Yes, and the free tier is generous. 18Birdies' help center says you can "use its core GPS, scoring, handicap, stats, shot tracking, and community features for free." That includes GPS distances to the green and hazards, a digital scorecard, handicap tracking, round history, multi-player scoring, side games, leagues, and core Apple Watch features.

Premium is a subscription: $99.99 a year or $19.99 a month with a 7-day free trial, or $7.99 a week with no trial (US prices, November 2026). It adds things like Plays Like distances for wind and slope, 3D Green Maps, Smart Tracking, an AI swing analyzer, and strokes gained.

## Can you score on Apple Watch with 18Birdies for free?

Yes. 18Birdies' App Store listing puts "APPLE WATCH: Distances, scoring, and shot tracking" in its free features, and its help center says core watch features don't need Premium. On the watch, Map View, Green Maps, Plays Like Distances, Auto Swing Detection, and Smart Tracking need Premium.

That's a real point in 18Birdies' favor. In {{ gc.short_name }}, the Apple Watch app is part of Pro, a one-time ${{ gc.pricing.pro_price }} (US price) after a {{ gc.pricing.trial_days }}-day free trial with no card. If free watch scoring with GPS is what you want, 18Birdies already gives you that.

## Should you switch from 18Birdies to {{ gc.short_name }}?

Only if you've found that you use 18Birdies mainly as a scorecard and want less around it. If you use its GPS, handicap tracking, group scoring, or community, stay with 18Birdies: {{ gc.short_name }} has none of those. Switching makes sense if you want no account, no GPS, and faster scoring.

Signs {{ gc.short_name }} may suit you better:

- **You only ever look at your score.** {{ gc.short_name }} opens straight to Quick Start, with no feed, no course search, and no sign-up.
- **You'd rather not share data.** {{ gc.short_name }}'s App Store privacy label says "Data Not Collected," and rounds stay in your own iCloud.
- **You want to score by voice or from the Lock Screen.** {{ gc.short_name }} Pro adds 8 [Siri commands](/apps/golf-caddie/guides/keep-score-in-golf-with-siri/) and a Lock Screen Live Activity.

## What does 18Birdies do that {{ gc.short_name }} doesn't?

Most things beyond the score. 18Birdies has free GPS yardages on 45,000+ courses, handicap tracking, shot tracking, multi-player scoring, side games, leagues and tournaments, a community feed, and swing playback. With Premium it adds Plays Like distances, green maps, and strokes gained. {{ gc.short_name }} offers none of these.

It's also one of the most-rated golf apps on the App Store, with a 4.9 rating from over 300,000 ratings in the US as of November 2026. If you want one app for yardages, handicap, and friends, it's a strong choice, and you can use most of it free.

## How do the privacy labels compare?

On the App Store, 18Birdies' developer-declared label lists Usage Data under "Data Used to Track You," and Location, Contact Info, Identifiers, Usage Data, and Diagnostics among "Data Linked to You." {{ gc.short_name }}'s label says "Data Not Collected." Both are declared by each developer, and Apple notes it hasn't verified them.

An app with GPS, friends, and leagues needs your location and an identity, so a longer label is expected. Read it before you download: scroll to "App Privacy" on the app's App Store page. Our guide to a [golf scorecard app with no account](/apps/golf-caddie/guides/golf-scorecard-app-no-account/) explains what each label means and where {{ gc.short_name }} stores your rounds.

## What about battery on Apple Watch?

GPS uses more battery than a scorecard. 18Birdies' App Store listing carries Apple's note that the app "may use your location even when it isn't open, which can decrease device battery life." {{ gc.short_name }} doesn't use location at all, so scoring taps on the watch are light on battery.

How long your watch lasts still depends on your model, its battery health, and whether a workout or Always On Display is running. Our [Apple Watch battery guide](/apps/golf-caddie/guides/apple-watch-battery-golf-round/) covers what drains it during a round and the settings that help.
