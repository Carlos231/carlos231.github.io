## Can Siri keep score in golf?

Yes, with a scorecard app that supports Siri. {{ gc.short_name }} has 8 Siri commands that start rounds, add strokes, putts, and penalties, move to the next hole, and tell you your score. They work on iPhone and Apple Watch without opening the app. Siri scoring is part of {{ gc.short_name }} Pro, and it's included in the free trial.

Every command needs "in {{ gc.short_name }}" at the end, so Siri knows which app to use. The table above lists each phrase exactly as the app understands it, with the variations it also accepts.

## How do you start a round with Siri?

Say "Start a quick round in {{ gc.short_name }}" to start an 18-hole round with every hole set to par 4. For nine holes, say "Start a front 9 holes round in {{ gc.short_name }}" or "Start a back 9 holes round in {{ gc.short_name }}". Siri confirms the round has started and wishes you good luck.

Quick Start rounds are named "Quick Round". If you want the course name and exact pars from the first hole, start the round in the app instead, then use Siri for the scoring.

## How do you add strokes, putts, and penalties by voice?

Say "Add a stroke in {{ gc.short_name }}" after each shot, "Add a putt in {{ gc.short_name }}" for each putt, and "Add a penalty in {{ gc.short_name }}" for a penalty stroke. Siri confirms each one with the running count, for example "Stroke added. Hole 4: 2 strokes." Say "Next hole in {{ gc.short_name }}" when you walk off the green.

The replies follow the same pattern:

- **Stroke:** "Stroke added. Hole 4: 2 strokes."
- **Putt:** "Putt added. Hole 4: 2 putts."
- **Penalty:** "Penalty added." followed by the hole and its penalty count.
- **Next hole:** "Moving to hole 5." On the last hole, Siri tells you you're already there.

As in the app, strokes on a hole lock once you've added a putt, so a stray "add a stroke" can't change a finished hole.

## How do you check your score with Siri?

Ask "What is my round score in {{ gc.short_name }}" for your total and where you stand against par, or "What is my hole score in {{ gc.short_name }}" for the current hole in golf terms like birdie or bogey. "What hole am I on in {{ gc.short_name }}" reads back the hole, its par, and what you've scored on it.

Example replies, in the format the app uses:

- **Round score:** "Quick Round: 13 total, 1 over par after 3 holes. Bogey."
- **Hole score:** "Hole 3: Bogey. 5 total, par 4."
- **What hole:** "Hole 4, par 4, 2 strokes, 1 putt, Birdie."

## Does Siri scoring work on Apple Watch?

Yes. The same commands work on Apple Watch, and your iPhone, Watch, and Lock Screen Live Activity stay in sync, so a stroke added by voice on your wrist shows up everywhere. Use Siri on the Watch the way you normally do. The {{ gc.short_name }} Watch app is part of Pro, like Siri scoring.

Siri works in the background on both devices, so you don't need to open {{ gc.short_name }} or look at a screen to add a stroke. For the full Watch setup, see [Apple Watch golf scorecard without GPS](/apps/golf-caddie/guides/apple-watch-golf-scorecard-without-gps/).

## When is voice faster than tapping?

Voice helps most when your hands are busy or your phone is in the bag: walking to the ball, wearing gloves, or riding in a cart. Say the command as you go and keep moving. Tapping is quicker when you're already looking at the screen, so most golfers mix both. Your scorecard is the same either way.
