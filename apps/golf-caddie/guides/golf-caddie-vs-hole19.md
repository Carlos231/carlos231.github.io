## Is Hole19 free?

Yes, much of it. As of November 2026, Hole19's free features include GPS distances to the front, center, and back of the green on 42,000+ courses, a digital scorecard, course previews, Apple Watch GPS, live leaderboards, and a golf social network. Premium is a subscription that adds shot tracking, stats, and more.

That's a strong free tier if GPS is why you want an app. Hole19 lists an "ad-free experience" under Premium, and it's built around distances, a community, and tracking as much as the scorecard.

## Can you score on Apple Watch with Hole19 for free?

Partly. As of November 2026, Hole19's Apple Watch page marks "Simple Scoring" as free and "Watch Scoring", described as "Full input on your wrist", as Premium. Its App Store listing also puts "Apple Watch Scoring & Maps" under Premium. GPS distances on the watch are free.

In {{ gc.short_name }}, the whole watch scorecard is part of Pro: strokes, putts, and penalties for every hole, your score vs par, and your club distances. Pro is a one-time ${{ gc.pricing.pro_price }} (US price) after a {{ gc.pricing.trial_days }}-day trial with no card, and it never renews.

## What does Hole19 Premium cost?

It's a subscription. As of November 2026, Hole19's website lists Premium at €69.99 a year or €14.99 a month, with a 14-day free trial, and a higher Intelligence tier at €99.99 a year. Its site shows euro prices, so check the App Store for the price in your country.

At the listed yearly price, three seasons of Premium come to about €210. {{ gc.short_name }} Pro is paid once. Scoring on iPhone is free with unlimited rounds, and Pro is a one-time ${{ gc.pricing.pro_price }} for the Apple Watch app, Siri, and the Lock Screen. See [what's free and what Pro adds]({{ gc.urls.page }}#pricing), or [what other golf apps cost](/apps/golf-caddie/guides/golf-app-prices-compared/).

## What does Hole19 do that {{ gc.short_name }} doesn't?

A lot. Hole19 gives you free GPS distances, course previews, live leaderboards, and a social network. Premium adds a shot tracker, a handicap calculator, club and overall stats, and club recommendations. Its Intelligence tier adds an AI caddie and Strokes Gained analysis. {{ gc.short_name }} has none of these.

If you want an app to tell you which club to hit, Hole19 can. {{ gc.short_name }} takes the opposite approach: you enter [your own club distances](/apps/golf-caddie/guides/how-to-find-your-club-distances/) once and read them off a cheat sheet. It's less clever and there's less to manage.

## When is {{ gc.short_name }} the better fit?

When you want a scorecard and nothing around it. {{ gc.short_name }} opens straight to Quick Start, with no account, no ads, no feed, and no AI features. You tap strokes, putts, and penalties and see your score vs par, on iPhone, on your wrist, by voice with Siri, or [from the Lock Screen](/apps/golf-caddie/guides/golf-scorecard-lock-screen/).

It also runs on iOS {{ gc.min_ios }} or later, while Hole19's listing asks for iOS 18.0 or later. If your iPhone can't update to iOS 18, that decides it.

## Which should you choose?

Stay with Hole19 if you use its GPS distances, course previews, or social features, or want shot tracking and an AI caddie. Choose {{ gc.short_name }} if you keep your own score, want full scoring on Apple Watch without a yearly plan, and prefer an app with no feed and no account.
