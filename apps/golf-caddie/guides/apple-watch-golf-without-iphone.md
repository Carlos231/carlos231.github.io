## Can you keep score on Apple Watch with your iPhone in the bag?

Yes. Start a round on the watch, tap your strokes, putts, and penalties on each hole, and swipe to the next one. Your iPhone can stay zipped in the golf bag the whole round. Every tap syncs to the iPhone, so the round is there when you take it out.

How a hole looks in {{ gc.short_name }}:

1. **Start the round.** Open {{ gc.short_name }} on the watch and pick a round from Quick Start. Or start it on your iPhone before you put it away; the watch picks it up.
2. **Score the hole.** Tap + for each stroke, putt, and penalty. Your total and score vs par update right away.
3. **Swipe to the next hole.** Repeat for every hole, then end the round from the last tab.

## What happens if the watch loses its connection to your iPhone?

Your scores aren't lost. {{ gc.short_name }} keeps the round on the watch, and if the iPhone is out of range, the changes are queued and sent once the two are connected again. With the iPhone in your golf bag, the two usually stay connected the whole round.

You don't need a cellular Apple Watch or a signal on the course. {{ gc.short_name }} doesn't use GPS or a course database, so there's nothing to download during the round.

## What still needs your iPhone?

Setup and anything after the round. You set your club distances, change a hole's par, and pick settings like Workout Tracking on the iPhone. You also start the Pro trial or buy Pro there. After the round, the iPhone is where you see stats, share a scorecard image, and export rounds.

| Task | Where |
|---|---|
| Start the free Pro trial or buy Pro | iPhone |
| Enter club distances | iPhone (shown on the watch) |
| Change a hole's par | iPhone (shown on the watch) |
| Settings, like Workout Tracking | iPhone (the watch follows) |
| Stats, sharing, and CSV export | iPhone |

## Will scoring on the watch drain your battery?

Not the way a GPS app does. {{ gc.short_name }} never tracks your location, so the watch isn't running GPS for four hours. Tapping in a score is a small cost. If you turn on a full Health workout, the heart rate sensor runs all round and uses more.

For the settings that matter most, see [Apple Watch battery for 18 holes](/apps/golf-caddie/guides/apple-watch-battery-golf-round/). If you're weighing a GPS app against a scorecard, [Apple Watch golf scorecard without GPS](/apps/golf-caddie/guides/apple-watch-golf-scorecard-without-gps/) covers what you give up.

## Can you score by voice instead of tapping?

Yes, with Pro. Raise your wrist and say "Add a stroke in {{ gc.short_name }}," "Add a putt in {{ gc.short_name }}," or "Next hole in {{ gc.short_name }}." Ask "What is my round score in {{ gc.short_name }}?" to hear where you stand. It's handy when you're wearing a glove or walking to the next tee.

The full list of phrases is in [keep score in golf with Siri](/apps/golf-caddie/guides/keep-score-in-golf-with-siri/). With Pro you can also score from your iPhone's Lock Screen, if you'd rather keep the phone in your pocket.
