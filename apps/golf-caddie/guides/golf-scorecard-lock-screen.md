## Can you keep golf score from the iPhone Lock Screen?

Yes. A Live Activity is the iPhone feature that keeps live information on your Lock Screen. A golf scorecard app that supports it shows your current hole there, with buttons you can tap while the phone stays locked. In {{ gc.short_name }}, it appears as soon as a round starts.

That changes how often you handle your phone. Instead of unlocking, finding the app, and tapping, you raise the phone, tap + once, and put it away. If your phone sits in a cart mount or your back pocket, it's the quickest way to count a shot.

## What can you do from the Lock Screen, and what needs the app?

You can do the scoring: add or take back strokes, putts, and penalties on the current hole, and move to the next hole. Starting a round, changing a hole's par, fixing an earlier hole, and ending the round happen in the app, or on Apple Watch where the watch supports it.

| Task | Lock Screen | In the app |
|---|---|---|
| Add or remove a stroke, putt, or penalty | Yes | Yes |
| Go to the next hole | Yes | Yes |
| See your score vs par | Yes | Yes |
| Start a round | No | Yes |
| Change a hole's par | No | Yes |
| Go back and fix an earlier hole | No | Yes |
| End the round | No | Yes |

One rule carries over from the scorecard: once you enter a putt on a hole, the stroke buttons lock. Strokes come before the green and putts on it, so the lock stops a stray tap from changing your stroke count after you've started putting.

## How do you set up Lock Screen scoring?

Start a round, and it's there. {{ gc.short_name }} shows the Live Activity on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island once a round is in progress. You need {{ gc.short_name }} Pro or an active Pro trial, and Live Activities have to be allowed for the app on your iPhone.

1. **Get Pro or start the free trial.** The trial is {{ gc.pricing.trial_days }} days and needs no card.
2. **Start a round.** Use Quick Start for 18 holes, the Front 9, or the Back 9, or name the course and set the pars.
3. **Lock your phone.** The Live Activity shows your current hole with + and – buttons.
4. **Tap as you play.** Each tap is saved right away, with the phone still locked.

When you end the round, the Live Activity closes on its own.

## Does it stay in sync with Apple Watch?

Yes. Every tap on the Lock Screen is saved and sent to your Apple Watch right away, even with the phone locked. Scores and hole changes you make on the watch show up on the Live Activity too. The next-hole button moves the scorecard, the Live Activity, and the watch together.

So you can mix them through a round: the watch while you walk, the Lock Screen when the phone is in the cart. On watchOS 11 or later, the Live Activity also appears in the watch's Smart Stack, and tapping it opens {{ gc.short_name }} on the watch.
