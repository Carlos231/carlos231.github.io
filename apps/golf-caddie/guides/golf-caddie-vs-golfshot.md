## Is Golfshot free?

Mostly, yes. As of November 2026, Golfshot's free version includes GPS distances on 47,000+ courses, scoring, shot tracking you enter yourself, statistics, 3D hole previews, Handicap Index linking, and Apple Watch GPS. The free version shows ads. Pro, $79.99 a year in the US, removes them and adds automatic tracking and deeper stats.

That's a generous free tier for golfers who want GPS. The trade-off is the ads, and an app built around yardages, tee times, and tracking rather than the scorecard alone.

## Can you keep score on Apple Watch with Golfshot for free?

Yes, according to Golfshot. Its October 2026 blog post says that on Apple Watch "it gives you GPS yardages and scoring for free." One caveat: its App Store listing still lists "Apple Watch scoring" under Pro, so check in the app if this decides it for you. Automatic shot and putt tracking on the watch need Pro.

In {{ gc.short_name }}, scoring on Apple Watch is part of Pro, a one-time ${{ gc.pricing.pro_price }} (US price) after a {{ gc.pricing.trial_days }}-day free trial with no card. What you get for it is a watch app that only keeps score: strokes, putts, and penalties, your score vs par, and your club distances.

## What does Golfshot Pro add, and what does it cost?

Automatic tracking and analysis. As of November 2026, Pro adds Auto Shot Tracking and Auto Putt Tracking on Apple Watch Series 8 or newer, Strokes Gained, Swing ID, Smart Caddy club recommendations, Green Maps, and no ads. It's $79.99 a year in the US, or $14.99 a month or $5.99 a week, with a 7-day free trial.

## How do the privacy labels compare?

On the App Store, Golfshot's developer-declared privacy label lists Location, Contact Info, Identifiers, and Usage Data under "Data Used to Track You," and its "Data Linked to You" includes Health & Fitness and Financial Info. {{ gc.short_name }}'s label says "Data Not Collected." Apple notes it hasn't verified either label.

An app with GPS, handicap posting, tee-time deals, and ads needs more data, so a longer label is expected. It's still worth a look before you download: scroll to "App Privacy" on any App Store page. Our guide to [golf scorecard apps with no account](/apps/golf-caddie/guides/golf-scorecard-app-no-account/) explains how to read it.

## What about battery on Apple Watch?

Golfshot says an Apple Watch with its app gets you through a full round, and that you'll charge it every night. Its App Store listing also notes that GPS running in the background on your iPhone "can dramatically decrease battery life." {{ gc.short_name }} doesn't use GPS at all, on the watch or the iPhone.

If your watch is older, or your battery health has dropped, that difference matters more. Our guide to [Apple Watch battery for 18 holes](/apps/golf-caddie/guides/apple-watch-battery-golf-round/) covers what drains it and the settings that help.
