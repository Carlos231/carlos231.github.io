## How much do golf app subscriptions cost?

As of November 2026, the main paid tier costs $59.99 a year for TheGrint Pro, $79.99 for Golfshot Pro, and $99.99 for 18Birdies Premium in the US. Arccos is $199.99 a year after the first year that comes with its hardware. All four renew automatically until you cancel.

Some also sell shorter plans:

- **18Birdies:** $19.99 a month or $7.99 a week. The weekly plan has no free trial.
- **Golfshot:** $14.99 a month or $5.99 a week. Its pages don't say which tier these cover.
- **TheGrint:** says its monthly price varies by country.

{{ gc.short_name }} doesn't have a subscription at all. The iPhone scorecard is free, and Pro is one payment of ${{ gc.pricing.pro_price }} (US price).

The subscription tiers pay for extra GPS tools, advanced stats, games, or shot tracking. If you use those every round, they can be worth it. If what you want from an app is your score, you're paying every year for features you don't open.

## Do you need a paid golf app to keep score?

No. Keeping score is free in {{ gc.short_name }}, with unlimited rounds on iPhone: strokes, putts, penalties, score vs par, stats, a shareable scorecard, and CSV export. The paid tiers of the big apps mostly add extra GPS tools, advanced stats, and games. If you just want your score, you don't need them.

## What does Apple Watch scoring cost?

It depends on the app, and it's often where the paywall sits. As of November 2026, TheGrint says "any score tracking requires Pro" on the watch ($59.99 a year). Golfshot's [October 2026 blog](https://golfshot.com/blog/do-you-need-a-golf-watch-or-just-a-golf-app) says watch scoring is free, and its Pro ($79.99 a year) adds automatic shot tracking. In {{ gc.short_name }}, watch scoring is part of Pro, which costs ${{ gc.pricing.pro_price }} once.

18Birdies also lists Apple Watch scoring in its free tier. One caveat on Golfshot: its App Store listing still puts "Apple Watch scoring" under Pro, so check in the app if you're deciding on Golfshot.

## Why pay once instead of every year?

Because nothing renews. A one-time purchase is yours to keep through app updates, never shows up in your subscriptions list, and doesn't charge you in January when the clubs are in the garage. {{ gc.short_name }}'s {{ gc.pricing.trial_days }}-day Pro trial starts in the app without a purchase, so there's nothing to cancel.

If you do keep a subscription, a monthly plan is cheaper than yearly when you play five months or fewer: 18Birdies is $19.99 a month vs $99.99 a year, and Golfshot $14.99 a month vs $79.99 for yearly Pro (US, November 2026).
