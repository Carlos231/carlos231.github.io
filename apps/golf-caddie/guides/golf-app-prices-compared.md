## How much do golf app subscriptions cost?

As of November 2026, the yearly price of the main paid tier ranges from $59.99 (TheGrint Pro) to $99.99 (18Birdies Premium) in the US, with Golfshot Pro at $79.99. Arccos is higher at $199.99 a year, but it's a shot tracking system with hardware, and the first year comes with the device.

Most of these apps also sell shorter plans:

- **18Birdies:** $19.99 a month or $7.99 a week. The weekly plan has no free trial.
- **Golfshot:** $14.99 a month or $5.99 a week. Its pages don't say which tier these plans cover.
- **TheGrint:** says its monthly price varies by country and points you to the App Store.

Every one of these renews automatically until you cancel it in your App Store account settings.

## Which golf apps let you keep score on Apple Watch for free?

Of the apps we checked, 18Birdies is the one whose App Store listing includes Apple Watch scoring in its free features, along with watch distances and shot tracking. TheGrint keeps watch GPS free but says "any score tracking requires Pro" on the watch. Golfshot's App Store listing puts Apple Watch scoring under Pro.

Golfshot is worth a closer look before you decide. Its US App Store listing lists "Apple Watch scoring" under what Pro unlocks, while its [facts page](https://golfshot.com/facts-about-golfshot) lists scoring and "Apple Watch support" in the free tier without saying whether that includes scoring on the watch. Check in the app during the free trial.

In {{ gc.short_name }}, scoring on the watch is part of Pro, which is a one-time ${{ gc.pricing.pro_price }} rather than a yearly fee. Scoring on iPhone is free with unlimited rounds.

These apps don't sell the same thing. TheGrint, Golfshot, and 18Birdies include GPS maps, detailed stats, and games in their paid tiers, and Arccos tracks every shot. {{ gc.short_name }} is a scorecard only. The fair question is which features you'd actually use, then what those cost.

## Is a free golf app enough?

For many golfers, yes. As of November 2026, TheGrint's free tier includes phone scoring, GPS on 40,000+ courses, and live leaderboards. 18Birdies' free tier includes GPS, a scorecard, handicap tracking, and Apple Watch scoring. {{ gc.short_name }} is free on iPhone with unlimited rounds, stats, and CSV export. Paid tiers add depth, not the basics.

Paying starts to make sense when you want something specific that the free tier doesn't include. Common reasons:

- **Scoring from your wrist** in apps that keep it in the paid tier.
- **Advanced stats** like Strokes Gained, which 18Birdies Premium, Golfshot Pro, and Arccos offer.
- **"Plays like" yardages** adjusted for slope and conditions, in 18Birdies Premium and Arccos.

If you mainly want to keep score, start with the free tier of whichever app you like best and only upgrade when you miss something.

## Is a monthly plan cheaper if you only play part of the year?

Often, yes. As of November 2026, 18Birdies Premium costs $99.99 a year or $19.99 a month in the US, so five months or fewer is cheaper monthly. Golfshot's yearly Pro is $79.99 and its monthly plan $14.99, so the line is also five months. Monthly plans renew until you cancel.

If you play a short season, put a reminder in your calendar to cancel the week your season ends. On iPhone, subscriptions are in **Settings → your name → Subscriptions**. TheGrint says its monthly price varies by country, so check the App Store for yours.
