## Why look for an Arccos alternative?

If cost is the reason, here's what you'd pay. As of November 2026, Arccos sells its game tracking through hardware: Smart Sensors for your clubs ($249.99), a Smart Laser Rangefinder ($299.99), or Arccos Air, a wearable for your pocket ($349.99). Each includes the first year of membership, and after that it's $199.99 a year in the US.

That's a lot to spend if what you really want is an accurate score. If you play a couple of times a month and just want to know how you shot, you don't need sensors on your clubs. You need a scorecard that's quick to use and stays out of the way.

## What does each cost over three seasons?

Three seasons with Arccos Smart Sensors cost about $650 in the US: $249.99 for the sensors with the first year included, then two years of membership at $199.99, if prices stay as they are in November 2026. {{ gc.short_name }} is free on iPhone, or ${{ gc.pricing.pro_price }} once for Pro, with nothing to renew.

## What do you give up without Arccos?

Automatic shot data. Arccos tracks every shot with its hardware and shows Strokes Gained, so you can see whether driving, approach, short game, or putting costs you the most. {{ gc.short_name }} records your strokes, putts, and penalties per hole, which tells you your score and your putting, but not where each shot went.

If you'd use shot-by-shot data to plan your practice, that's what the Arccos membership pays for. If you mostly want your score, your putts, and your trend over a season, {{ gc.short_name }}'s free stats cover it, and you can [export every round to a spreadsheet](/apps/golf-caddie/) for your own analysis.

## Can you get your club distances without Arccos?

Yes. Hit 10 balls with each club on the range, drop the longest and shortest few, and write down the typical carry of the rest. Confirm the numbers on the course over a few rounds. It takes an afternoon, and the numbers are yours to keep.

The [Rules of Golf (Rule 4.3a)](https://www.randa.org/en/rog/the-rules-of-golf/rule-4) let you use club distances you gathered before the round. In {{ gc.short_name }}, you enter them once under Settings → Club Distances, and they're one tap from the scorecard on iPhone, and on your wrist with Pro. Our guide on [how to find your club distances](/apps/golf-caddie/guides/how-to-find-your-club-distances/) walks through the routine.
