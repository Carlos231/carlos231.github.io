## How much does Arccos cost?

As of November 2026, Arccos sells three ways in: Smart Sensors for your clubs ($249.99), a Smart Laser Rangefinder ($299.99), and Arccos Air, a wearable that goes in your pocket ($349.99). Each includes the first year of membership. After that, the membership is $199.99 a year.

The hardware is how Arccos knows where each shot went. Arccos says the Air needs nothing on your clubs, and that the Smart Laser needs "No Sensors, No Game Tracking." The pages we checked don't list a monthly price or an app-only plan, so if that's what you want, check with Arccos.

## What does Arccos do that {{ gc.short_name }} doesn't?

Almost everything beyond the score itself. Arccos tracks every shot automatically, works out your club distances from real shots, and shows Strokes Gained across driving, approach, short game, and putting. Its app adds shot dispersion charts, "plays like" yardages adjusted for wind and slope, and club and target recommendations.

If you want to know exactly where you lose strokes, that's Arccos's home ground. {{ gc.short_name }} records strokes, putts, and penalties per hole and shows your score vs par. It doesn't know where your ball went.

Pick Arccos if one of these is the reason you'd use a golf app:

- **You want data without writing anything down.** Arccos captures every shot on its own once the hardware is set up.
- **You practice with a plan.** Strokes Gained by facet tells you whether to work on driving, approach, short game, or putting.
- **You want measured club distances.** Arccos builds them from your tracked shots instead of your memory.

## When is {{ gc.short_name }} the better fit?

When you only want to keep an accurate score with as little gear as possible. {{ gc.short_name }} needs no sensors, no wearable, and no account. You open it and start a round. Score from your iPhone, from your wrist on Apple Watch, by voice with Siri, or from the Lock Screen.

What you get in {{ gc.short_name }}:

- **Free on iPhone:** unlimited rounds, strokes, putts, penalties, score vs par, stats, a shareable scorecard, CSV export, club distances, and Apple Health golf workouts.
- **Pro, one-time ${{ gc.pricing.pro_price }}:** the Apple Watch app, 8 [Siri voice commands](/apps/golf-caddie/guides/keep-score-in-golf-with-siri/), and a Lock Screen Live Activity. Try it free for {{ gc.pricing.trial_days }} days with no card.

## Can you get Arccos-style club distances without Arccos?

Yes, with some effort. Hit 10 balls with each club on a range or launch monitor, note the typical carry (not your best one), and confirm the numbers on the course over a few rounds. Arccos does this automatically from tracked shots. Doing it yourself takes an afternoon and a notepad.

The [Rules of Golf (Rule 4.3a)](https://www.randa.org/en/rog/the-rules-of-golf/rule-4) let you use club distances you gathered before the round, so a written list is fine in competition. Our guide on [how to find your club distances](/apps/golf-caddie/guides/how-to-find-your-club-distances/) walks through the full routine. Once you have the numbers, keep them on a card, in your phone, or in {{ gc.short_name }}'s Club Distances.

## Which should you choose?

Pick Arccos if you want automatic shot tracking, Strokes Gained, and measured club distances, and you'll use them to practice. Pick {{ gc.short_name }} if you keep your own score, want to do it from your watch or by voice, and would rather not buy hardware or renew a membership.

You can also use both. Arccos can track your shots while {{ gc.short_name }} keeps a simple scorecard you can share or export to a spreadsheet. Not sure you need GPS at all? Read [Apple Watch golf scorecard without GPS](/apps/golf-caddie/guides/apple-watch-golf-scorecard-without-gps/).
