Three ways to score without your phone in your hand

Scoring on iPhone is free. {{ gc.short_name }} Pro adds these three, and they all stay in sync.

From your wrist

Tap + for strokes, putts, and penalties on Apple Watch and swipe to the next hole. Your iPhone can stay in the bag.

By voice

Say “Add a stroke in {{ gc.short_name }}” or “Next hole in {{ gc.short_name }}” to Siri. See every phrase that works.

From the Lock Screen

A Live Activity puts + and – buttons on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island, so you can count a stroke without unlocking.

How it works

  1. Start on your watch or iPhone

    Quick Start a full 18, the Front 9, or the Back 9. There's no sign-up and no course to download.

  2. Score each hole your way

    Tap on your wrist, ask Siri, or use the Lock Screen. Every device shows the same hole and the same score vs par.

  3. Finish and look back

    End the round to see your summary, share a classic scorecard, and follow your scoring trends on iPhone.
