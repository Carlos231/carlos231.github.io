## Wednesday: read the privacy label before you download

**Angle:** a practical check any golfer can do on any app. Links to the no-account guide.

**X**
> Before you download a golf app, scroll to "App Privacy" on its App Store page. It shows what's used to track you, what's linked to your identity, or "Data Not Collected." Takes ten seconds. How to read it: https://carlos231.github.io/apps/golf-caddie/guides/golf-scorecard-app-no-account/

**Threads** (topic: Apple)
> A ten-second check before installing any golf app: open its App Store page and scroll to App Privacy. Apple makes every developer list what they collect, in four groups: Data Used to Track You, Data Linked to You, Data Not Linked to You, and Data Not Collected. Apps with GPS, friends, and handicaps need more data, which can be fine. Just know before you sign up. Guide in replies.

## Friday: 34 putts doesn't mean what you think

**Angle:** the worked example in the putts guide (made-up card, labeled as an example).

**X**
> An example card: 34 putts. Sounds average. Split it by greens hit: 17 putts on 7 greens, and 17 on the 11 greens missed. Every three-putt started 35+ feet away. The fix is lag putting, not short putts. How to track it: https://carlos231.github.io/apps/golf-caddie/guides/how-to-track-putts/

**Threads** (topic: Golf)
> Putts per round can hide what's going on. In the example card from my putts guide, 34 putts looks ordinary. Split it by greens hit in regulation and you get about 2.4 putts per green hit, while chips onto missed greens kept the rest low. All three three-putts started 35 feet or more away. Record putts, green hit yes or no, and a rough first-putt distance. Guide in replies.

**Instagram / TikTok caption**
> Your putt count can lie to you. A good chip leaves a one-putt and makes your total look better than your putting. Track three things per hole: putts, whether you hit the green in regulation, and roughly how far your first putt was. After a few rounds you'll see whether to practice lag putting or short ones. Worked 18-hole example in bio.
>
