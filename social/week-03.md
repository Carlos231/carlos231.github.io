## Tuesday: Low Power Mode on the course

**Angle:** Apple's three steps, and what it turns off.

**X**
> Low Power Mode on Apple Watch turns off Always On Display, background heart rate readings and heart rate notifications. On the course that's an easy trade. Side button, tap the battery percentage, Low Power Mode. Full guide: https://carlos231.github.io/apps/golf-caddie/guides/apple-watch-battery-golf-round/

**Threads** (topic: Apple Watch)
> A battery tip for golfers with an Apple Watch. Low Power Mode turns off Always On Display, background heart rate and blood oxygen readings, and heart rate notifications, and it limits some connections. Press the side button, tap the battery percentage, then tap Low Power Mode. "Turn On For" lets you pick a duration, so a few hours covers a round. Apple notes some notifications may be delayed. Guide in replies.
